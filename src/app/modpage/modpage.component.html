Trials of the Eldritch

Mod Summary

In Trials of the Eldritch, players become the chosen envoy of forgotten Eldritch gods, ancient and enigmatic beings who offer power and knowledge at a price. The world is fraught with peril as arcane magic twists the land, and you are tasked to undertake contracts, forge mythical weapons, and confront trials that test your will, intelligence, and might.

Core Lore

Millennia ago, the Eldritch gods were banished beyond the Veil, a boundary separating their chaotic realm from the mortal plane. They slumbered in silence, waiting for the right envoy to heed their whispers and wield their might once more. Now, through a mystical artifact—the Rune Table—the gods speak to you. This ancient instrument bridges realms, allowing you to decipher their cryptic instructions, receive contracts, and channel their arcane powers.

Each contract you undertake deepens your connection to the gods, but it also comes with a cost. You may gain unimaginable power, but the gods are not benevolent; every boon comes with a trial, and every trial carries the weight of your soul.

Features

  • The Rune Table: A sacred, glowing stone slab inscribed with shifting, ancient runes. Players use it to interact with the Eldritch gods, accept contracts, and unlock runes that grant spells and enchantments.
  • Eldritch Contracts: Complete tasks dictated by the gods—ranging from hunting mythical beasts to solving arcane puzzles. Each contract advances your standing and unlocks powerful rewards, but failing a trial can anger the gods.
  • Mythical Weapons: Forge powerful, rune-inscribed weapons infused with the essence of the gods. These weapons grow stronger as you succeed in trials, but misusing them risks catastrophic consequences.
  • Challenging Opponents: Encounter Eldritch guardians, cursed beings, and rival envoys. Each opponent has unique abilities tied to their own Eldritch god, making combat dynamic and strategic.
  • Familiars: Bind mystical creatures to your service. These familiars are extensions of the gods' will and assist in battles, exploration, and solving puzzles.
  • Corruption System: Balancing the power of the gods and your mortal essence is key. Relying too heavily on their gifts risks corruption, altering your character and the world around you.

Thematic Elements

  • Mystery and Danger: The gods’ motives are unclear, and their true intentions are never fully revealed.
  • Moral Dilemmas: Some contracts force you to choose between power and your humanity. Will you honor the gods or defy them?
  • Evolving Rune Table: As you complete trials, the Rune Table changes, unlocking new runes and revealing more of its mysterious history.
